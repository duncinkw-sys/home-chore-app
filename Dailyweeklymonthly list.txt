Daily Chores (Upkeep & Mess Prevention)Kitchen: Wash or rinse dishes, run/empty the dishwasher, wipe down countertops, and clear the table.Surfaces & Tidy-up: Make beds, declutter shared living spaces, and do a quick wipe of bathroom sinks/faucets.Floors: Sweep up quick crumbs or use a robot vacuum.Trash: Empty and take out full garbage, recycling, and compost bins.Pets: Feed pets, provide fresh water, and clean litter boxes or cages.Weekly Chores (Deep Clean & Maintenance)Bathrooms: Scrub and disinfect toilets, tubs, and showers. Clean mirrors and polish fixtures.Floors: Vacuum all rugs and carpets. Sweep, mop, or steam hard floors.Laundry: Wash, fold, and put away clothes. Wash bath towels, dish towels, and bed linens.Kitchen Deep Dive: Disinfect the kitchen sink, clean the microwave, wipe down major appliances, and clean out the fridge to toss expired food.Dusting: Dust shelves, tables, and window sills.Monthly/Seasonal Chores (Occasional Upkeep)Appliances: Clean out the oven, defrost/deep clean the freezer, and run a cleaning cycle on the washing machine.Air Quality: Change HVAC or furnace filters and vacuum vents.Windows & Blinds: Dust blinds, wash curtains or upholstery, and clean interior/exterior windows.Closets & Pantry: Organize seasonal clothing and deep clean the pantry by checking expiration dates.
